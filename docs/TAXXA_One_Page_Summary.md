## What is TAXXA?

A **mobile lottery platform** that incentivizes citizens to request receipts, dramatically increasing tax compliance and government revenue.

**How it works:** Consumer requests receipt → Scans QR code → Receipt validated with Tax Authority → Enters prize draw
